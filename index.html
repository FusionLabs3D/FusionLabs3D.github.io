// 04 • EXECUTION PROTOCOL

Engineering-Grade Prototyping Workflow

Every step is calibrated for physical repeatability and dimensional integrity.

01

CAD Validation & Slicing

Direct ingestion of .STEP, .STL, or .OBJ files. Parametric geometry is analyzed for wall thickness, anisotropic layer orientation, and thermal shrinkage allowance.

02

Filament Dehydration

Hygroscopic polymers (PA-CF, PAHT-CF, PPA-CF, PETG) undergo active moisture dehydration before extrusion to guarantee zero steam micro-voids and maximum tensile strength.

03

Enclosed Thermal Printing

Parts are fabricated using hardened nozzles (0.4mm & 0.6mm) inside temperature-stabilized chambers to eliminate residual thermal stress and corner warping.

04

Inspection & Heat-Set Inserts

Calipers and bore gauges verify key dimensions. If required, heat-set brass threaded inserts (M2–M6) and post-print annealing cycles are completed.
