ORDER WORKFLOW

How to Order Your 3D Prints

Simple, direct, and completely transparent — from initial file review to doorstep delivery.

01 STEP 1

Send Your Model

Share your .STL, .STEP, .3MF, or a community link (MakerWorld / Printables) directly via WhatsApp or Instagram DM.

02 STEP 2

Transparent Quote

We slice your part in Orca Slicer to calculate exact total material weight (including supports and purge) and machine time baseline. No hidden markups.

03 STEP 3

Print & Photo QC

Printed on enclosed Bambu Lab printers with dehydrated filament. Once inspected, we send multi-angle photos for your 3-day approval window.

04 STEP 4

Pan-India Dispatch

Upon final approval, parts are packaged with protective cushioning and desiccant packs, then dispatched via Speed Post / Blue Dart with tracking.

Transparent Manufacturing Terms: Our 50% upfront deposit policy, Orca Slicer machine-time formula (≤7h baseline / 100g), standard FDM tolerances (±0.2–0.3mm), and photo-approval criteria are clearly defined.
